# How Audiofy compares

Audiofy is a free music app for Android that puts JioSaavn and YouTube in one search and one player, with synced lyrics, offline downloads and no ads. This page says what that means next to the other ways people listen on Android, and who should pick something else. Back to the [README](README.md).

## At a glance

| | Audiofy |
|---|---|
| Price | Free |
| Ads | None |
| Account | Not needed |
| Music sources | JioSaavn and YouTube, searched together |
| Lyrics | Time-synced, in the original, Hinglish, Hindi or English |
| Offline | Yes, songs saved with cover and lyrics |
| Playlists with friends | Blend, shared by link or QR code |
| Looks | Four player designs, themes, sound presets |
| Platforms | Android 7.0 and newer only |
| Install | APK from this repository or the website, not the Play Store |

## Audiofy and using JioSaavn and YouTube separately

Audiofy does not replace JioSaavn or YouTube. It reads from both.

- **One search.** You look once and see results from both catalogs, so the film song, the indie cover and the live version appear together.
- **One player and one library.** Likes, downloads and playlists live in one place instead of two apps.
- **Extras on top.** Synced lyrics in the language you pick, Blend playlists, Daily Mixes and a sound engine with equalizer and reverb.
- **Sound quality.** Songs from JioSaavn can play up to 320 kbps. Songs from YouTube play at the quality YouTube serves.
- **Dependence.** Audiofy relies on what those catalogs allow. If a source changes how it works, Audiofy may need an update before that source plays again.

## Audiofy and paid streaming subscriptions

- **Cost.** Audiofy is free. Paid subscriptions charge a monthly fee. Check each service's own page for its current price and features.
- **Catalog.** Audiofy's catalog is what JioSaavn and YouTube offer. It is strong for Hindi, Punjabi, Bhojpuri and English music.
- **Install and support.** Paid apps come from the Play Store and have a company behind them. Audiofy is an independent app installed from an APK.

## Who Audiofy is for

- People who listen to Hindi, Punjabi, Bhojpuri and English songs and want one app
- People who like reading synced lyrics while they listen
- People who want to save songs for offline listening without paying
- Friends who want to share one playlist

## Who should pick something else

- **iPhone users.** There is no iPhone version yet.
- **Anyone who needs the Play Store.** Audiofy is installed from an APK, and Android shows an unknown app prompt first.
- **Anyone who needs guaranteed support or a company contract.** Audiofy is independent and free.

Audiofy is not affiliated with JioSaavn or YouTube. Other apps change often, so verify their current features on their own pages. Questions about Audiofy are answered in the [FAQ](FAQ.md), and every version is listed in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
