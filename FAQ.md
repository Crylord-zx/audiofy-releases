# Audiofy FAQ

Answers about Audiofy, the free music app for Android. Back to the [README](README.md) or the [website](https://audiofy-music.vercel.app).

## The basics

### What is Audiofy?
Audiofy is a free music app for Android. It searches JioSaavn and YouTube together, plays songs in one player, and adds synced lyrics, offline downloads, smart autoplay and shared playlists. It has no ads and needs no account.

### Is Audiofy free?
Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

### Who is it for?
Anyone who listens to Hindi, Punjabi, Bhojpuri, English and other music and wants one app instead of switching between several. It also suits people who want lyrics, downloads and a clean player without paying.

### Is there an iPhone version?
Not yet. Audiofy is Android only for now.

### Does it need an account or sign-up?
No. Your library, likes and downloads stay on your phone.

## Install and update

### How do I install Audiofy?
Download the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/Crylord-zx/audiofy-releases/releases/latest), open it, allow your browser to install unknown apps if Android asks, then tap Install and Open.

### Which file should I download?
Use `audiofy-arm64-v8a.apk` for nearly every phone from the last several years. Use `audiofy-armeabi-v7a.apk` only on an older 32-bit phone.

### Which Android versions are supported?
Android 7.0 and newer.

### Why does Android warn me before installing?
Android shows an unknown app prompt for any app that does not come from the Play Store. Audiofy is published only on its website and in this repository, so the prompt is expected. If Play Protect offers to scan the app, tap Scan app. If it shows More details, you can choose Install anyway.

### How can I check that my download is genuine?
Each [release page](https://github.com/Crylord-zx/audiofy-releases/releases) shows a SHA-256 hash next to every file. Compare it with the hash of the file you downloaded. Updates only install over an existing Audiofy when they are signed with the same key.

### How do I update?
Audiofy tells you when a new version is out. Tap Update now, wait for the download, then tap the Download complete notification and choose Install. You can also open Settings, then Check for updates. Your library and downloads are kept.

## Using the app

### Which music sources does Audiofy use?
JioSaavn and YouTube. Search shows both together, and you can choose which source you prefer.

### How do the lyrics work?
Lyrics are time-synced and scroll with the song. You can read them in the original language, Hinglish, Hindi or English, change the style, and share a line as a card.

### Can I listen offline?
Yes. Downloaded songs are saved with their cover and lyrics and play with no internet.

### What is Blend?
Blend mixes your taste with a friend's into one shared playlist. You share it with a link or a QR code.

### What are Daily Mixes and Discover Weekly?
Playlists Audiofy builds from what you play. They learn from what you finish and skip, and you can switch between Familiar and Discover or keep a private session that is not remembered.

### Can I change how the app looks?
Yes. There are four player designs, several looks and home styles, and a sound engine with equalizer, reverb and other presets.

### Does Audiofy play in the background?
Yes. Music keeps playing with notification and lock-screen controls.
