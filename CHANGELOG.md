# Changelog

All notable changes to Audiofy, newest first. Downloads for every version are on the [releases page](https://github.com/Crylord-zx/audiofy-releases/releases).

## 1.3.2

- Share Audiofy: one tap in Settings sends your friends the download link
- Smoother everywhere: pages no longer overlap when you move around, and changing your look now fades in
- Share with a QR code: show your Blend invite as a QR and a friend scans it with their camera
- Lyric cards in two styles: a bold Spotify-style card and a frosted Apple-style card
- Cleaner sound: safer equalizer stacking, softer reverb and a truer Nightcore
- Search stays put: recent searches no longer vanish while you scroll

Fixes

- No more black screen when changing the preferred source
- Instruments label in Mix balance no longer wraps
- Removed the Hi-Res Lossless source option
- Slimmer About page
